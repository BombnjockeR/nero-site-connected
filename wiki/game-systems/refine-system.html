Blacksmith Blessing (BSB)

  • Works only with Normal or HD Elunium.
  • Adds +5% success rate.
  • Your gear won't break or drop below +7 while using it.
    • 1 Blacksmith Blessing is required to refine from +4 to +5.
    • 2 Blacksmith Blessings are required to refine from +5 to +6.
    • 3 Blacksmith Blessings are required to refine from +7 to +8.
    • 4 Blacksmith Blessings are required to refine from +9 to +10.
